Check In Together

Emergency safety check-ins and alerts for people living alone

Check In Together is an emergency check-in service that notifies designated emergency contacts via SMS during urgent situations. Users complete regular safety check-ins, and missed check-ins trigger automatic alerts to their chosen emergency contacts.

📱 Who Sends Messages

Messages are sent by Tynan Seltzer operating Check In Together, a personal safety monitoring service based in the United States.

✉️ Non-Marketing Service

No promotional messages are sent. Emergency contacts only receive:

  • Initial opt-in consent requests (one-time)
  • Emergency alerts when a user misses check-ins
  • No advertisements, promotions, or marketing materials
Learn More Opt-In Process

How It Works

Check In Together requires explicit consent before sending any emergency alerts.

1

User Adds Emergency Contact

A Check In Together user (the person living alone) adds an emergency contact's phone number in the app. The contact is typically a family member, friend, or neighbor.

2

Opt-In Request Sent

The emergency contact receives a one-time SMS asking them to opt in. This message clearly explains what they're consenting to and how to respond.

Example Opt-In SMS:
"[John Smith] listed you as an emergency contact for Check In Together. Reply YES to receive emergency alerts if they miss safety check-ins. Reply STOP to opt out. Msg&data rates may apply. Help: support@checkintogetherapp.com"
3

Contact Must Reply YES

The emergency contact must explicitly reply with "YES" to opt in. No alerts will be sent until consent is confirmed. We store this consent record with timestamp, phone number, and consent text.

4

Emergency Alerts (Rare)

Only after consent, the contact may receive emergency alerts if the user misses consecutive check-ins.

Example Emergency Alert:
"🚨 Emergency alert from Check In Together: [John Smith] has missed 2 consecutive safety check-ins. Last check-in: Jan 15, 2026 9:00 AM. Please contact them immediately to ensure they are safe. Reply STOP to opt out."

📊 Message Frequency

Extremely Rare - Emergency contacts only receive messages during actual emergencies when a user misses consecutive check-ins. In normal operation, contacts may receive zero messages per month. This is not a frequent notification service.

Consent & Opt-In Process

This is the official consent documentation page for Twilio toll-free verification.

Explicit Opt-In Requirement

Check In Together requires explicit, prior express consent from all emergency contacts before sending any SMS messages. Here's how we obtain and verify consent:

1. Consent Method

  • SMS Reply: Emergency contacts receive an initial SMS requesting consent and must reply "YES" to opt in
  • User-initiated: The primary user (person being monitored) initiates the consent request by adding the contact in our app
  • Clear language: The opt-in message explicitly states what they're consenting to receive

2. Exact Opt-In Wording

SMS sent to emergency contact:

"[User Name] listed you as an emergency contact for Check In Together. Reply YES to receive emergency alerts if they miss safety check-ins. Reply STOP to opt out. Msg&data rates may apply. Help: support@checkintogetherapp.com"

3. What "YES" Means

By replying "YES," the emergency contact explicitly consents to:

  • Receive rare emergency alert SMS messages when the user misses safety check-ins
  • Be contacted via SMS during urgent situations requiring wellness checks
  • Standard messaging and data rates from their carrier

They do NOT consent to:

  • Marketing messages (none are ever sent)
  • Promotional content
  • Frequent or regular messages
  • Sharing their information with third parties

4. Consent Record Storage

When an emergency contact opts in, we store the following as proof of consent:

  • Phone number of the emergency contact
  • Timestamp of when they replied "YES"
  • Consent text - the exact opt-in message they received
  • Method - SMS reply confirmation
  • User association - which user added them as an emergency contact
  • IP address (if opt-in via web) - not applicable for SMS opt-in

These records are retained for the duration of the emergency contact relationship and for 3 years after opt-out for compliance purposes.

SMS Policy

🛑 STOP - Opt-Out

Reply STOP to opt out at any time.

Emergency contacts can opt out immediately by replying STOP to any message. When you opt out:

  • You will no longer receive emergency alerts for that user
  • Your opt-out is processed immediately (within seconds)
  • You will receive one final confirmation message: "You have been unsubscribed from Check In Together emergency alerts. No further messages will be sent."
  • The primary user will be notified to add a different emergency contact

We honor all STOP requests immediately and automatically. No human intervention is required.

❓ HELP - Get Help

Reply HELP for assistance.

If you reply HELP, you'll receive:

"Check In Together: Emergency alerts for safety check-ins. STOP to opt out. Questions? Email support@checkintogetherapp.com"

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 24, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Check In Together collects the following information:

From Primary Users (People Being Monitored):

  • Name
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Email address (optional)
  • Check-in preferences (frequency, time)
  • Check-in history and timestamps

From Emergency Contacts:

  • Phone number
  • Name (provided by primary user)
  • Opt-in consent timestamp and method
  • Message delivery status (sent, delivered, failed)

Automatically Collected:

  • Device type and operating system (for app functionality)
  • App usage data (check-in completion, settings changes)
  • SMS delivery receipts and status

Device Permissions:

  • Contacts: Used only when you tap "Import from Contacts" to select an emergency contact. We only store the name and phone number of contacts you explicitly choose. Your contact list is not uploaded or scanned.
  • Notifications: Used to remind you when a check-in is due.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Check-in tracking: Track check-in completion and trigger alerts to your designated contacts when you miss check-ins
  • Emergency notifications: Send SMS alerts to opted-in emergency contacts
  • Consent management: Store and honor opt-in/opt-out preferences
  • Service improvement: Analyze usage patterns (anonymized) to improve reliability
  • Support: Respond to user inquiries and technical issues

3. Who We Share Your Information With

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Service Providers:

  • Supabase - Backend hosting: stores your account data, emergency contact opt-in status, and schedule for server-side reminder escalation. Provides authentication and serverless functions.
  • Twilio - SMS delivery service (data processor only, does not retain messages)
  • SendGrid - Email delivery service (if email alerts are enabled)
  • These providers are bound by data processing agreements and cannot use your data for their own purposes

Legal Requirements:

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of users in genuine emergency situations.

With Consent:

Emergency alerts are only sent to contacts you explicitly designate and who have opted in.

4. Data Retention

  • Active users: Data retained while account is active
  • Check-in history: Retained for 12 months for safety analysis
  • Consent records: Retained for 3 years after opt-out for compliance
  • After account deletion: All personal data deleted within 30 days (except consent records required for legal compliance)

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correct: Update inaccurate information in the app settings
  • Delete: Request account deletion and data removal
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from SMS alerts at any time (reply STOP)
  • Data portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format

To exercise these rights, email: support@checkintogetherapp.com
