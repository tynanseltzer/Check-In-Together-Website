Check In Together

Emergency safety check-ins and alerts for people living alone

Check In Together is an emergency check-in service that notifies designated emergency contacts via SMS during urgent situations. Users complete regular safety check-ins, and missed check-ins trigger automatic alerts to their chosen emergency contacts.

📱 Who Sends Messages

Messages are sent by Check In Together LLC, which operates Check In Together, a check-in service based in the United States.

✉️ Non-Marketing Service

No promotional messages are sent. Emergency contacts only receive:

  • Initial opt-in consent requests (one-time)
  • Emergency alerts when a user misses check-ins
  • No advertisements, promotions, or marketing materials
Learn More Opt-In Process

How It Works

Check In Together requires explicit consent before sending any emergency alerts.

1

User Adds Emergency Contact

A Check In Together user (the person living alone) adds an emergency contact's phone number in the app. The contact is typically a family member, friend, or neighbor.

2

Opt-In SMS Sent with Keyword

The emergency contact receives an SMS text message asking them to opt in by replying with the keyword "YES". The message says who listed them, what they will be told, and how to decline.

Opt-In SMS Text Message:
"[John Smith] listed you as an emergency contact for Check In Together. Reply YES to be told if they miss their scheduled check-ins. Reply STOP to decline. Msg&data rates may apply. Help: support@checkintogetherapp.com"
3

Contact Replies "YES" to Consent

The emergency contact must explicitly reply with the keyword "YES" to agree to receive SMS text messages. No text messages will be sent until this SMS consent is confirmed. We store the consent record: the phone number, the time they replied YES, and that they consented by text reply.

4

Emergency Alert SMS (Rare)

Only after SMS consent is confirmed, the contact may receive an alert text message if the person misses a scheduled check-in and has not checked in within the waiting time chosen in the app.

Example Emergency Alert SMS:
"ALERT from Check In Together: [John Smith] has not checked in for about [3 hours]. Please check on them."

📊 Message Frequency

Extremely Rare - Emergency contacts only receive messages when a person misses a check-in, or in the other cases listed under SMS Consent below. In normal operation, contacts may receive zero messages per month. This is not a frequent notification service.

SMS Consent & Opt-In Process

Opt-in type: VIA_TEXT — Emergency contacts, and people who receive check-ins set up by someone else, opt in by replying with the keyword "YES" via SMS.

SMS Text Message Consent

By replying "YES" to our opt-in request, you agree to receive SMS text messages from Check In Together at the phone number provided. Messages include:

  • A message if they tap "Alert my contacts" in the app
  • A short heads-up if their answers to an in-app check-in suggest it's worth reaching out (for example, they mentioned a fall)

Message frequency: Rare — only when one of the events above happens (typically 0-2 messages per month).

How the VIA_TEXT Opt-In Works

Check In Together uses a keyword-based SMS opt-in (VIA_TEXT) to obtain explicit consent from emergency contacts before sending any text messages. Below is the complete opt-in workflow:

1. Opt-In Workflow (Step by Step)

Step 1

User adds emergency contact in app: A Check In Together user (the person living alone) enters an emergency contact's phone number in the Check In Together mobile app. The contact is typically a family member, friend, or neighbor.

Step 2

System sends opt-in SMS with keyword: Within a few minutes, the emergency contact receives an SMS text message from our toll-free number asking them to opt in by replying with the keyword "YES". This message explains what they will be told and how to decline.

Step 3

Contact replies "YES" to consent: The emergency contact must reply with the keyword "YES" to explicitly consent to receiving SMS text messages. No other text messages will be sent to the contact until they reply YES. If they do not reply, we send the same request once more about three days later, and then nothing further.

Step 4

Confirmation SMS sent: After replying YES, the contact receives a single confirmation SMS text message verifying their enrollment and reminding them they can reply STOP at any time to opt out.

2. How Contacts Learn About the Keyword

Emergency contacts learn about the opt-in keyword through the initial SMS text message sent by our system. When a Check In Together user adds an emergency contact in the app, the system automatically sends the contact an SMS that contains the keyword "YES" and instructions to reply. The contact does not need to know the keyword in advance — it is provided directly in the opt-in request SMS they receive.

3. Exact Opt-In SMS Wording

Opt-in request SMS sent to emergency contact:

"[User Name] listed you as an emergency contact for Check In Together. Reply YES to be told if they miss their scheduled check-ins. Reply STOP to decline. Msg&data rates may apply. Help: support@checkintogetherapp.com"

Confirmation SMS sent after contact replies YES:

"You're confirmed to receive emergency alerts from Check In Together when your contact misses safety check-ins. Reply STOP anytime to opt out."

4. What Replying "YES" Means — Explicit SMS Consent

By replying "YES," the emergency contact explicitly agrees to receive SMS text messages from Check In Together. Specifically, they consent to:

  • Receive SMS text messages alerting them when the person who listed them misses a scheduled check-in, and the related messages listed above
  • Receive a one-time confirmation SMS text message after opting in
  • Standard message and data rates may apply from their mobile carrier

They do NOT consent to:

  • Marketing or promotional text messages (none are ever sent)
  • Sharing their phone number with third parties
  • Frequent or regularly scheduled messages

5. Consent Record Storage

When an emergency contact opts in by replying YES, we store the following as proof of SMS consent:

  • Phone number of the emergency contact
  • Timestamp of when they replied "YES"
  • Opt-in method — SMS reply (only a reply starting with "YES" or "Y" is recorded as consent)
  • User association — which user added them as an emergency contact
  • Opt-out — the time they replied STOP, if they do

The opt-in request itself is recorded in our SMS provider's (Twilio's) message log. Consent records are kept with the contact's entry in the account and are deleted when the account holder removes that contact or deletes their account. Separately, our SMS provider blocks all further texts from our number to a number that has replied STOP.

7. Opt-In for People Who Receive Check-Ins

An account holder can set up check-ins for someone else, such as a parent. That person must also opt in by replying "YES" before we send them any check-in texts or place any check-in calls. Within a few minutes of being added, they receive the text below. If they do not reply, we send it once more about three days later, and then nothing further.

Opt-in request SMS sent to the person being checked in on:

"[Account Holder Name] set up Check In Together to check in on you with a brief regular text conversation. Reply YES to start, or STOP to decline. Tip: save this number as "Check In Together" so our check-ins always reach you. Msg&data rates may apply. Help: support@checkintogetherapp.com"

For check-ins by phone, "a brief regular text conversation" reads "a brief regular call".

Confirmation SMS sent after they reply YES:

"You're all set - Check In Together will reach out for your regular check-ins. Reply STOP anytime to opt out."

After they reply YES, they receive their check-ins on the schedule set in the app (typically once a day): either a short phone call, or a short text conversation that begins:

"Hi [Name], it's your Check In Together check-in. [First question] (Reply STOP to opt out.)"

If a check-in goes unanswered, we try again later that day. They are also sent a notice if their check-ins are paused because they have not responded for three days. Replying STOP ends all check-in texts and calls to them. Message and data rates may apply.

People who set up check-ins for themselves choose calls or texts in the app and enter their own number; they are not sent a consent text.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 25, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Check In Together collects the following information:

Accounts:

There is no username, password or email sign-up. The app signs in with an anonymous account created on your phone, and everything below is linked to that account.

From Primary Users (People Being Checked In On):

One account can be set up for the account holder, or by a caregiver for one or more people they look after. For each person checked in on we collect:

  • Name
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Check-in preferences (method, time, and how long to wait before alerting contacts)
  • When check-ins were completed or missed (the answers and history of in-app check-ins stay on your phone)

From Emergency Contacts:

  • Phone number
  • Name and relationship (provided by primary user)
  • Opt-in consent timestamp and method, and the time of any opt-out
  • Whether our texts to them were accepted for delivery, and any error code

Consent texts to emergency contacts and to people being checked in on are sent by our servers, at most twice per request, and only for people the account holder added and asked us to contact. If a contact added in an earlier app version was saved only on the phone, updating the app saves them with us and sends that one consent text.

Automatically Collected:

  • Your phone's time zone, so check-ins happen at your local time
  • App usage data (check-in completion, settings changes)
  • The status of our texts and calls (for example, whether a call was answered, went to voicemail, or failed)
  • Standard request logs kept by our hosting providers, which can include IP addresses

Device Permissions:

  • Contacts: Used only when you tap "Import from Contacts" to select an emergency contact, or "Save our number to your contacts" to add Check In Together's number to your phone. We only store the name and phone number of contacts you explicitly choose. Your contact list is not uploaded or scanned.
  • Notifications: Used to remind you when a check-in is due.

Check-In Calls, Texts and Answers:

  • Voice check-ins are placed as a regular phone call to the number you provide. The call audio is carried by Twilio and relayed through our voice server (hosted by Fly.io) to OpenAI in real time, so the AI companion can converse naturally and the call can be transcribed.
  • Text check-ins are a back-and-forth series of SMS messages: we text a few check-in questions and read the replies. The message content is processed to run the check-in.
  • A text transcript of the voice call or text conversation is stored on our servers so the account holder can see a short summary in the app, and is processed by our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate it. Your responses can include wellbeing or health-related details. You can request deletion of your transcripts at any time by emailing support@checkintogetherapp.com.
  • In-app check-ins: your answers are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to write a short summary shown in the app, to update the recap described below, and to decide whether your opted-in emergency contacts should get a short heads-up text describing what was noticed (for example, that you mentioned a fall).
  • We also keep a short, evolving written recap of how the person has been over time — their usual routine, things they've mentioned, and anything worth following up on — built from their check-in responses and updated after each check-in. We retain your check-in responses to maintain this recap, and it is included when our AI provider (Anthropic) summarizes your next check-in, so the recap reflects how you've been over time rather than a single day. It is kept while your account is active and can be deleted on request.
  • The check-in companion is not a doctor and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or health monitoring. Check-ins are intended as friendly conversations, not assessments.

4. Who We Share Your Information With

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Service Providers:

  • Supabase - Backend hosting (United States): stores your account data, emergency contacts and their opt-in status, your check-in schedule for server-side reminders and alerts, voice and text check-in transcripts and summaries, and the running recap. Provides authentication (the anonymous accounts described above) and serverless functions.
  • Twilio - SMS and voice call delivery (data processor only). For voice check-ins, Twilio carries the audio of the call between you and our AI companion and detects whether a person or voicemail answered; Twilio does not record or retain call audio for us.   • Anthropic (Claude) - After each check-in, the transcript (voice or text check-ins) or your answers (in-app check-ins) are sent to Anthropic to generate a short plain-language summary, to maintain the running recap described above (which can include wellbeing/health-related details shared over prior check-ins), and, for in-app check-ins, to decide whether to send your emergency contacts a heads-up. Anthropic does not train on this data per their API terms.
  • These providers are bound by data processing agreements and cannot use your data for their own purposes.

Legal Requirements:

With Consent:

5. Data Retention

  • Active users: Data retained while account is active
  • Check-in transcripts, summaries and the recap: Kept while the account is active, and deleted when the account is deleted or on request. In-app check-in history is stored on your phone.
  • Consent records: Kept with the contact's (or checked-in person's) entry in the account, and deleted when the account holder removes them or deletes the account. Our SMS provider separately keeps blocking texts to any number that replied STOP.
  • After account deletion: Deleting your account in the app (Settings → Delete Account) immediately and permanently removes your check-in schedule, check-in history and summaries, the running recap, your emergency contacts, and the account itself — and stops all check-in texts and calls and all alerts to your emergency contacts. Deletion requests made by email are completed within 30 days. Deletion does not reach records held outside our database: our providers' own records, such as Twilio's logs of texts and calls already sent, and short-lived server logs are kept under those providers' retention periods.
  • Uninstalling the app: Does not delete your account or its data, and does not stop check-in calls and texts or alerts to your emergency contacts. Delete your account in the app first (Settings → Delete Account), or email us.
