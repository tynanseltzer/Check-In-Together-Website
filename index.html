Check In Together

Emergency safety check-ins and alerts for people living alone

Check In Together is an emergency check-in service that notifies designated emergency contacts via SMS during urgent situations. Users complete regular safety check-ins, and missed check-ins trigger automatic alerts to their chosen emergency contacts.

📱 Who Sends Messages

Messages are sent by Tynan Seltzer operating Check In Together, a personal safety monitoring service based in the United States.

✉️ Non-Marketing Service

No promotional messages are sent. Emergency contacts only receive:

  • Initial opt-in consent requests (one-time)
  • Emergency alerts when a user misses check-ins
  • No advertisements, promotions, or marketing materials
Learn More Opt-In Process

How It Works

Check In Together requires explicit consent before sending any emergency alerts.

1

User Adds Emergency Contact

A Check In Together user (the person living alone) adds an emergency contact's phone number in the app. The contact is typically a family member, friend, or neighbor.

2

Opt-In SMS Sent with Keyword

The emergency contact receives an SMS text message asking them to opt in by replying with the keyword "YES". This message clearly explains that they are consenting to receive SMS text messages and describes what messages they will receive.

Opt-In SMS Text Message:
"[John Smith] listed you as an emergency contact for Check In Together. By replying YES, you agree to receive SMS text messages with emergency safety alerts if they miss check-ins. Msg frequency: rare (emergencies only). Msg&data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. HELP for help. support@checkintogetherapp.com"
3

Contact Replies "YES" to Consent

The emergency contact must explicitly reply with the keyword "YES" to agree to receive SMS text messages. No text messages will be sent until this SMS consent is confirmed. We store the consent record including timestamp, phone number, keyword used, and the opt-in message text.

4

Emergency Alert SMS (Rare)

Only after SMS consent is confirmed, the contact may receive emergency alert text messages if the user misses consecutive check-ins.

Example Emergency Alert SMS:
"🚨 Emergency alert from Check In Together: [John Smith] has missed 2 consecutive safety check-ins. Last check-in: Jan 15, 2026 9:00 AM. Please contact them immediately to ensure they are safe. Reply STOP to opt out."

📊 Message Frequency

Extremely Rare - Emergency contacts only receive messages during actual emergencies when a user misses consecutive check-ins. In normal operation, contacts may receive zero messages per month. This is not a frequent notification service.

How the VIA_TEXT Opt-In Works

Check In Together uses a keyword-based SMS opt-in (VIA_TEXT) to obtain explicit consent from emergency contacts before sending any text messages. Below is the complete opt-in workflow:

1. Opt-In Workflow (Step by Step)

Step 1

User adds emergency contact in app: A Check In Together user (the person living alone) enters an emergency contact's phone number in the Check In Together mobile app. The contact is typically a family member, friend, or neighbor.

Step 2

System sends opt-in SMS with keyword: The emergency contact immediately receives an SMS text message from our toll-free number asking them to opt in by replying with the keyword "YES". This message clearly explains what text messages they will receive and how to opt out.

Step 3

Contact replies "YES" to consent: The emergency contact must reply with the keyword "YES" to explicitly consent to receiving SMS text messages. No text messages will be sent to the contact until they reply YES. If they do not reply, no further messages are sent.

Step 4

Confirmation SMS sent: After replying YES, the contact receives a single confirmation SMS text message verifying their enrollment and reminding them they can reply STOP at any time to opt out.

2. How Contacts Learn About the Keyword

Emergency contacts learn about the opt-in keyword through the initial SMS text message sent by our system. When a Check In Together user adds an emergency contact in the app, the system automatically sends the contact an SMS that contains the keyword "YES" and instructions to reply. The contact does not need to know the keyword in advance — it is provided directly in the opt-in request SMS they receive.

3. Exact Opt-In SMS Wording

Opt-in request SMS sent to emergency contact:

"[User Name] listed you as an emergency contact for Check In Together. By replying YES, you agree to receive SMS text messages with emergency safety alerts if they miss check-ins. Msg frequency: rare (emergencies only). Msg&data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. HELP for help. support@checkintogetherapp.com"

Confirmation SMS sent after contact replies YES:

"You've opted in to receive SMS text messages from Check In Together. You'll receive emergency alerts if [User Name] misses safety check-ins. Msg frequency: rare. Msg&data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out anytime. HELP for help."

4. What Replying "YES" Means — Explicit SMS Consent

By replying "YES," the emergency contact explicitly agrees to receive SMS text messages from Check In Together. Specifically, they consent to:

  • Receive SMS text messages containing emergency safety alerts when the user misses consecutive check-ins
  • Receive a one-time confirmation SMS text message after opting in
  • Standard message and data rates may apply from their mobile carrier

They do NOT consent to:

  • Marketing or promotional text messages (none are ever sent)
  • Sharing their phone number with third parties
  • Frequent or regularly scheduled messages

5. Consent Record Storage

When an emergency contact opts in by replying YES, we store the following as proof of SMS consent:

  • Phone number of the emergency contact
  • Timestamp of when they replied "YES"
  • Consent text — the exact opt-in SMS message they received
  • Opt-in method — VIA_TEXT (SMS keyword reply)
  • Keyword used — "YES"
  • User association — which user added them as an emergency contact

These records are retained for the duration of the emergency contact relationship and for 3 years after opt-out for compliance purposes.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Check In Together collects the following information:

From Primary Users (People Being Monitored):

One account can be set up for the account holder, or by a caregiver for one or more people they look after. For each person checked in on we collect:

  • Name
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Email address (optional)
  • Check-in preferences (frequency, time)
  • Check-in history and timestamps

From Emergency Contacts:

  • Phone number
  • Name (provided by primary user)
  • Opt-in consent timestamp and method
  • Message delivery status (sent, delivered, failed)

Consent texts to emergency contacts and to people being checked in on are sent by our servers, at most twice per request, and only for people the account holder added and asked us to contact. If a contact added in an earlier app version was saved only on the phone, updating the app saves them with us and sends that one consent text.

Automatically Collected:

  • Device type and operating system (for app functionality)
  • App usage data (check-in completion, settings changes)
  • SMS delivery receipts and status

Device Permissions:

  • Contacts: Used only when you tap "Import from Contacts" to select an emergency contact. We only store the name and phone number of contacts you explicitly choose. Your contact list is not uploaded or scanned.
  • Notifications: Used to remind you when a check-in is due.

For Users Who Choose Voice or Text Check-Ins:

  • Voice check-ins are placed as a regular phone call to the number you provide. The call audio is processed by Twilio and OpenAI in real time so the AI companion can converse naturally.
  • Text check-ins are a back-and-forth series of SMS messages: we text a few check-in questions and read the replies. The message content is processed to run the check-in.
  • A text transcript of the voice call or text conversation is stored on our servers so we can give you and your emergency contacts a short summary, and is processed by our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate it. Your responses can include wellbeing or health-related details. You can request deletion of your transcripts at any time by emailing support@checkintogetherapp.com.
  • We also keep a short, evolving written recap of how the person has been over time — their usual routine, things they've mentioned, and anything worth following up on — built from their check-in responses and updated after each check-in. We retain your check-in responses to maintain this recap, and it is included when our AI provider (Anthropic) summarizes your next check-in, so the recap reflects how you've been over time rather than a single day. It is kept while your account is active and can be deleted on request.
  • The check-in companion is not a doctor and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or health monitoring. Check-ins are intended as friendly conversations, not assessments.

Check In Together sends SMS text messages to emergency contacts. We obtain explicit consent via text message (VIA_TEXT) before sending any messages:

  • Messages sent: Emergency safety check-in alerts and one-time opt-in confirmation
  • Message frequency: Rare — only during emergencies (typically 0-2 per month)
  • Opt-out: Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving SMS text messages
  • Help: Reply HELP for assistance or email support@checkintogetherapp.com

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number with any third parties. Your phone number is used solely for sending emergency safety alerts via SMS.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Check-in tracking: Track check-in completion and trigger alerts to your designated contacts when you miss check-ins
  • Emergency notifications: Send SMS alerts to opted-in emergency contacts
  • Consent management: Store and honor opt-in/opt-out preferences
  • Service improvement: Analyze usage patterns (anonymized) to improve reliability
  • Support: Respond to user inquiries and technical issues

4. Who We Share Your Information With

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Service Providers:

  • Supabase - Backend hosting: stores your account data, emergency contact opt-in status, schedule for server-side reminder escalation, and (for users who opt into voice check-ins) call transcripts and summaries. Provides authentication and serverless functions.
  • Twilio - SMS and voice call delivery (data processor only). For voice check-ins, Twilio carries the audio of the call between you and our AI companion; Twilio does not record or retain call audio for us.
  • OpenAI - Real-time voice conversation engine for users who choose voice check-ins. Audio of the call is sent to OpenAI to drive the conversation. OpenAI does not train on this data per their API terms.
  • Anthropic (Claude) - After each check-in, the transcript is sent to Anthropic to generate a short plain-language summary an emergency contact could read, and to maintain the running recap described above (which can include wellbeing/health-related details shared over prior check-ins). Anthropic does not train on this data per their API terms.
  • SendGrid - Email delivery service (if email alerts are enabled)
  • These providers are bound by data processing agreements and cannot use your data for their own purposes.

Legal Requirements:

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of users in genuine emergency situations.

With Consent:

Emergency alerts are only sent to contacts you explicitly designate and who have opted in.

5. Data Retention

  • Active users: Data retained while account is active
  • Check-in history: Retained for 12 months for safety analysis

  • After account deletion: Deleting your account in the app (Settings → Delete Account) immediately and permanently removes your check-in schedule, check-in history and summaries, the running recap, your emergency contacts, and the account itself — and stops all check-in texts and calls and all alerts to your emergency contacts. Deletion requests made by email are completed within 30 days. Consent records required for legal compliance may be retained.
