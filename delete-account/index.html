Delete your account

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Check In Together account, what is deleted, and what we keep. Back to the main page

If you have the app

  1. Open Check In Together.
  2. Tap Settings at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the message, then tap Delete.

Deleting your account cannot be undone.

Removing the app from your phone does not delete your account. Check-ins and alerts keep going until you delete the account in the app or ask us to delete it.

If you reinstalled the app or changed phones, the earlier install made a separate account. Deleting your account in the app deletes only the current one. Email us to delete the earlier one.

If you no longer have the app

Email us at support@checkintogetherapp.com and ask us to delete your account. You can write from any email address.

Please include one of these, so we can find the account:

  • the phone number that receives the check-ins, or
  • the phone number of one of the account's emergency contacts.

If you check in only in the app, please give an emergency contact's number.

Accounts have no login or password, so we cannot find an account from a name alone.

Before we delete anything, we check that the request comes from the account holder. We reply to your email and ask for details of the account that its holder would know, such as the check-in time and the numbers of its emergency contacts. If we cannot confirm the request, we do not delete the account, and we tell you so by email. Once it is confirmed, we delete the account within 30 days.

If an account has no phone number and no emergency contact, we cannot find it. Delete it in the app.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • your check-in schedule
  • your check-in history and summaries
  • your check-in profile (the running recap)
  • your emergency contacts
  • the account itself

All check-in texts and calls to you stop, and your emergency contacts no longer receive any alerts.

What we keep

If the account had a subscription: we keep the subscription's records after the account is deleted, for as long as we operate the service. These are the store's transaction identifier, the deleted account's random ID and the date it was deleted, and the notices the store sent us about the subscription (which plan, its dates, its price, and the store's country). They hold no name, phone number or email address. The store keeps sending us notices until the subscription ends. We store them, and we do not apply them to any account.

Delivery records: we keep technical records of texts and calls we tried to send. They hold the account's random ID, times, and whether sending worked. They hold no name, no phone number and no message text. Most are deleted within 90 days. The record that a request to reply YES was sent is kept for as long as we operate the service. For up to two days we also keep scrambled codes of the numbers we last texted.

If you remove one person but keep your account: that person's check-in history, calls, replies and profile are deleted. For as long as your account exists, we keep one short record of the removal. It holds no name and no phone number. It holds a scrambled code made from the phone number, which we use only to recognise the same number if it is added again, and dates and counts: when the person was removed, how many texts asking them to reply YES were sent to that number and when the last one was sent, whether they had replied YES, whether they had replied STOP, and whether adding them had switched off check-ins to the same number on another account. If we had written a short note about that person after a false alarm, it is kept until the account is deleted.

Records held by the companies that send our texts and calls are described in our Privacy Policy.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription

If you have a subscription, the subscription continues until you cancel it there. We cannot cancel it for you.

  • iPhone or iPad: open Settings, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  • Android: cancel it in Google Play.

You can cancel before or after deleting your account.

If you only want the texts to stop

If you are an emergency contact, or someone set up check-ins for you, you do not need an account or the app. Reply STOP to any of our texts, at any time.
